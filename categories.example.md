# Categories

Pick exactly one category per email. The `## ` headings must match the `categories` list in config.toml.

## research

Coauthors and collaborators, papers and preprints, research projects, code and R packages, grants and proposals, students asking about a thesis or a research project.

Examples:
- A coauthor sends new simulation results and asks to check Section 4 before Friday → research, needs action.
- A PhD student sends a thesis chapter draft → research, needs action.
- GitHub notification about an issue on one of the owner's repositories → research, no action unless someone asks the owner directly.

## teaching

Courses, students asking about a course, the e-learning platform, exams, grades, syllabi, department teaching coordination.

Examples:
- A student asks whether topic 5 is in Tuesday's exam → teaching, needs action.
- The department asks for the teaching preferences for next year by a date → teaching, needs action, deadline.

## admin

University administration: invoices and facturas, payments, expense justifications, purchasing, contracts, HR, IT and servers, forms and signatures.

Examples:
- Accounting asks for signed invoices of a trip before 15 October → admin, needs action, deadline.
- IT announces a password change is required for the owner's account → admin, needs action.
- The invoice of a conference the owner organizes → admin (not events).

## events

Conferences, workshops and seminars the owner organizes or attends: registrations, speakers, travel, venues, camera-ready papers, invitations to give a talk.

Examples:
- An author of the conference the owner organizes sends the camera-ready paper → events.
- A colleague invites the owner to give a seminar → events, needs action.
- The hotel confirms the owner's booking for a workshop → events, no action.

## reviewing

Referee and editorial requests, review reminders, program committee work.

Examples:
- A journal invites the owner to review a manuscript → reviewing, needs action.
- Reminder that a review is overdue → reviewing, needs action.

## info

FYI only: newsletters, broadcasts, mailing lists, notifications, calls with no personal ask.

Examples:
- A call for papers sent to a mailing list → info (not events), no action, even if it has a deadline.
- A university broadcast about an open day → info.
- A seminar announcement sent to the whole department → info.

## Edge cases

- Event logistics are events, the same event's invoices and justifications are admin.
- PhD matters go by subject: thesis research is research, a PhD student's courses are teaching, enrolment, committees, forms and funding paperwork are admin.
- Anything sent to a list or to many people with no personal request is info.
